⚡ Safe performance checklist

Before playingClose unused apps; keep enough storage free; enable Do Not Disturb if you want.
In-game graphicsIf the game lags, try Smooth graphics and a stable frame-rate option available on your device.
TemperatureAvoid playing while charging if the phone heats up; let it cool down.
Touch controlsUse normal system touch settings. Avoid unknown booster, macro, or cheat apps.
NetworkUse a stable trusted Wi-Fi or mobile connection. A VPN cannot guarantee lower ping.
